# Yinne Marketplace

Marketplace is an optional discovery channel for products already published in your Yinne Storefront. Opt in by accepting Marketplace terms, verifying your contact, and creating a public merchant profile. Create a listing for an eligible canonical product, choose a category and optional public title/description, then submit it for moderation.

Only approved, eligible listings appear publicly. Product prices, variants, availability, inventory, checkout, and payment continue to come from your canonical Yinne commerce data. Pausing a Store, unpublishing a product, disabling its variants, running out of tracked stock, or suspending a listing prevents new purchases.

Marketplace checkout contains products from one merchant only. Marketplace does not provide reviews, recommendations, lending, escrow, split payments, payouts, or Marketplace fees in this phase.
