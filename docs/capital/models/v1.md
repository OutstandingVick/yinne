# Capital model `rules-1`

## Intended use

`rules-1` summarizes operational stability visible within Yinne for merchant self-understanding and future partner integration evaluation. It is not a credit decision, loan eligibility result, probability of default, offer, or financing recommendation.

## Configuration

- Scale: integer 0–100, half-up rounding.
- Window: trailing 180 complete local days.
- Required observations: 90 days and 30 paid orders.
- Minimum observable base weight: 70.
- Currency: one ISO currency, normally the organization default; no FX.
- Weights: consistency 25, growth 20, cash-flow stability 20, customer quality 15, refund risk 10, operating history 10.
- Bands: 0–39 `limited`, 40–59 `developing`, 60–79 `stable`, 80–100 `highly_stable`.

Exact signal formulas and threshold knots are in `docs/capital/signal-catalogue.md` and mirrored by the immutable source configuration. Linear interpolation is used between knots and outputs are clamped to 0–100.

## Missing data

Required signal absence yields `insufficient_data`; it never contributes zero. Customer quality becomes not applicable when buyer count or identity coverage is inadequate. Applicable dimensions are proportionally reweighted only when at least 70 base-weight points remain observable.

## Sufficiency

- `insufficient`: scoring minimums are not met.
- `limited`: minimums met, but fewer than 120 observed days or 50 paid orders.
- `sufficient`: at least 120 days and 50 paid orders.
- `strong`: full 180-day observation, at least 100 paid orders, and customer identity coverage of at least 80%.

## Excluded inputs

Protected personal characteristics, geography as a risk proxy, social/external data, subscription usage, invoice usage, number of locations, provider identity, and customer-level PII are excluded. Subscription-led and invoice-led revenue still appears through canonical collected volume without receiving a separate score boost.

## Historical stability

Snapshots retain model version, configuration version, window, currency, raw aggregate evidence, normalized scores, weights, contributions, explanations, and limitations. A future model change uses a new identifier; it does not rewrite `rules-1` history.

## Limitations

Yinne cannot observe external sales, expenses, liabilities, settlement timing, bank balances, taxes, fraud losses, seasonality beyond the window, or economic context. Scores may be affected by incomplete identity attribution and by merchants routing only some activity through Yinne. Thresholds are heuristic and require partner-specific validation before any regulated use.
