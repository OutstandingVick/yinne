## Contribution and reweighting

Applicable base weight must be at least 70. Effective weight is `base_weight / sum(applicable_base_weights) × 100`; contribution is `normalized_score × effective_weight / 100`. Final score is the half-up rounded sum of contributions. Customer quality is the only optional V1 signal. No subscription, invoice, or location usage is rewarded or penalized.

## Heuristic status

All normalization knots are transparent product heuristics derived from the approved planning specification, not empirically calibrated default-risk estimates. Any threshold change creates a new model version and cannot alter stored historical snapshots.
