# Phase 6 UX and accessibility review

Reviewed Locations, Employees, Invoice list/create/detail, and public Invoice payment at desktop and
responsive layouts. Pages use semantic headings, labelled tables and controls, readable status text,
integer-derived formatted amounts, visible busy/error states, and native keyboard-operable elements.

Invoice detail communicates lifecycle, due date, amount, and payment correlation. Public Invoice
shows Merchant identity, number, items, total, due state, and a single clear payment action. Paid and
non-payable states remove that action and communicate completion in text rather than color alone.

Result: PASS for Phase 6 scope. PDF invoices, localization, automated reminders, and advanced screen
reader announcements for asynchronous provider updates remain deferred.
