# Phase 9 bias and fairness review

Result: pass, subject to final full regression.

The model uses aggregate merchant operational facts only. Protected personal characteristics, personal credit, external/social data, provider identity, and geography are absent. Missing customer identity is not negative evidence. New merchants receive insufficient status instead of a bad score. Subscription, invoice, and multi-location adoption are neither required nor rewarded. Thresholds are labelled heuristic, intended use is restricted, historical evidence is reviewable, and any model change requires a new version and renewed fairness review.
