# Phase 8 UX and Accessibility Review

Result: pass, subject to final browser verification.

Analytics has first-class navigation, descriptive headings, semantic tables, status text, formula/freshness/window context, and explicit empty/not-comparable labels. Currency is visible and separated rather than hidden behind a preferred-currency total. Metrics use plain business language and explain that processed volume is not accounting revenue. Tables remain the accessible representation; no chart is the sole carrier of information.
